# Purpose

The Relationship Layer gives every relationship a permanent home inside Meetro.

It does not replace Leads.

It does not replace Current Work.

It does not replace Work Center.

It connects them.

Every relationship continues here.

---

# The Philosophy

People remember relationships.

Software usually remembers projects.

Meetro remembers both.

Projects begin.

Projects end.

Relationships continue.

The Relationship Layer exists to preserve that continuity.

# Contact

A Contact is the beginning of a relationship.

A Contact is not yet an active Meetro participant.

Contacts may be:

Customers

Tenants

Property Managers

Businesses

Professionals

Employees

Vendors

Property Contacts

Importing creates Contacts.

Inviting activates Meetro participation.

---

# Conversation

Conversation is the living expression of a relationship.

Messages is the home of conversations.

Conversation should never be interrupted unnecessarily.

Everything that requires collaboration should naturally emerge from conversation.

---

# Relationship

A Relationship is the permanent record connecting people through work.

A relationship may contain:

Conversations

Current Work

Projects

Quotes

Invoices

Payments

Documents

Photos

History

Relationship Memory

Future Follow-ups

The relationship remains after every project ends.

---

# Current Work

Current Work is temporary.

Relationships are permanent.

Current Work belongs to a relationship.

Work Center organizes Current Work.

Messages preserves the relationship.

# Messages

Messages is not the product.

Messages is the interface through which relationships continue.

Messages should answer one question:

Who do I want to continue talking to?

Not:

What project do I want to manage?

Work belongs to Work Center.

Relationships belong to Messages.
