# Purpose

Business DNA allows Meetro to understand the world a business operates in before learning the unique characteristics of that individual business.

It does not define the business.

It establishes the context in which understanding begins.

The Understanding Engine learns the business.

Business DNA provides the starting point.

---

# Philosophy

No two businesses operate in exactly the same world.

A property manager and a handyman both coordinate work.

But they coordinate different relationships.

Business DNA allows Meetro to reveal only what naturally belongs to that business.

The platform remains one.

The experience adapts.

---

# The Principle

Business Identity determines what belongs.

Understanding determines how it evolves.

Subscription determines what remains enabled.

These responsibilities must remain separate.

---

# Business Identity

Every business begins by answering one simple question:

**What best describes your business?**

This is not a capability survey.

It is not a services list.

It is an identity.

Identity becomes Business DNA.

# Subscription

Business DNA determines eligibility.

Subscription determines availability.

Example:

Property Management DNA

↓

Property Workspace appears

↓

10-day trial begins

↓

Subscription enables continued use.

The product should never advertise irrelevant subscriptions.

Subscriptions should appear only when they belong to the business.

---

# Growth

Businesses evolve.

The Understanding Engine observes that evolution.

Example:

A general contractor begins managing multiple apartment complexes.

Meetro notices recurring property management behavior.

It may suggest:

"We've noticed your business now coordinates multiple properties and recurring tenant work.

Would you like to explore the Property Management Workspace?"

The business confirms.

Business DNA evolves.

---

# The Understanding Engine

Business DNA provides context.

The Understanding Engine provides understanding.

Business DNA never replaces observation.

Observation always remains the stronger source of truth.

---

# Design Principles

Reveal only what belongs.

Hide unnecessary complexity.

Allow Business DNA to shape the experience quietly.

Allow the Understanding Engine to refine that experience over time.

Never require businesses to configure what Meetro can responsibly learn.

---

# The Founder's Vision

Meetro should never become a platform with every feature visible to everyone.

Each business should feel that Meetro was built specifically for the work it performs and the relationships it manages.

The platform remains unified.

The experience becomes personal.

Business DNA protects that experience.

---

# Closing

Business DNA is not a limitation.

It is the beginning of understanding.

The Understanding Engine continues learning.

Relationships continue growing.

The business continues evolving.

The platform evolves with it.

Protect this architecture.

Because it allows one platform to serve many industries without becoming cluttered, confusing, or fragmented.
