# Meetro Community Guidelines

Our goal is to help professionals and customers build strong, respectful, productive relationships.

## Users Should

- Communicate respectfully
- Provide accurate information
- Honor commitments
- Act professionally
- Use honest reviews and feedback
- Respect privacy

## Users May Not

- Harass or threaten others
- Engage in discrimination or hate speech
- Submit fraudulent requests
- Misrepresent qualifications
- Manipulate reviews
- Upload unlawful content
- Abuse emergency services

Violations may result in account restrictions, suspension, or removal.

Meetro encourages constructive communication, accountability, transparency, and respect throughout the relationship lifecycle.
