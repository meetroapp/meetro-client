# Business Operations District Wonder Pass

## Purpose

Business Operations helps professionals build and operate a healthy business.

This district includes Business Dashboard, Business Tools, Business Profile,
Availability, Hiring Center, Pricing Library, Quote Builder, Invoice Builder,
Contracts, Reports, Business Leads, Business Knowledge, and Readiness.

The district should feel like different rooms inside the same professional
workshop, not different applications.

## Visual Adoption

Business Operations uses the Meetro Community visual constitution:

- meetro-visual-page
- meetro-visual-hero
- meetro-visual-surface
- meetro-visual-primary-button
- meetro-visual-empty-state
- forest and deep forest action language
- sage, cream, paper, wood, and coffee support tones
- warm borders
- soft shadows

Purple may remain only as a minor accent. It must not define primary actions,
hero treatments, or operational status language.

## Information Hierarchy

Apply the Law of Cognitive Calm:

- primary work should be obvious first
- reference content should be grouped quietly
- desktop space should increase breathing room, not compress the workspace
- operational CTAs should be forest, not purple
- read-only tools should feel like business records, not app demos

## Rooms

Business Dashboard is the daily operating desk.

Business Tools is the workshop index.

Business Profile and Portfolio are public trust rooms.

Availability is operational readiness.

Hiring Center is team-building.

Pricing Library, Contract Templates, Reports, Asset Center, Compliance Center,
and Business Intelligence are business knowledge and records rooms.

Quote Builder and Invoice Builder are preparation surfaces. Their calculations,
save paths, and delivery paths remain owned by their existing workflows.

## Guardrails

- Do not change backend behavior.
- Do not change routes.
- Do not change database contracts.
- Do not change pricing calculations.
- Do not change invoice calculations.
- Do not change hiring logic.
- Do not change availability truth.
- Do not change language.js for this visual pass.

## Recommendation

Future passes should continue replacing isolated legacy accent styling with
shared visual tokens, but only after confirming the surface is part of Business
Operations and the color is not carrying warning, destructive, or selected-media
meaning.
