# Meetro Community Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meetro collects, uses, stores, and protects information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Profile information
- Business information
- Messages and communications
- Photos and documents
- Project information
- Device information
- Usage information
- Location information when authorized

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate the platform
- Facilitate communication
- Support workflows
- Improve user experience
- Provide customer support
- Enhance security
- Generate analytics
- Support AI-assisted features

## 3. Communication Data

Messages, attachments, project records, and communications may be stored to support platform functionality and history features.

## 4. Location Data

Location information may be used for service matching, emergency workflows, scheduling, and related platform functions when permission is granted.

## 5. AI Features

Information provided by users may be processed by AI systems to generate recommendations, summaries, workflow guidance, and related assistance.

Users remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated content.

## 6. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that support platform operations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 7. Data Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect information; however, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention

Information may be retained as necessary to operate the platform, preserve history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain records.

## 9. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of information where permitted by applicable law.

## 10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

## 11. Contact

Questions regarding privacy practices may be directed through official Meetro support channels.
