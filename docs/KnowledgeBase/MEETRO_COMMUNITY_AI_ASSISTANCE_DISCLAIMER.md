# Meetro Community AI Assistance Disclaimer

Last Updated: June 2026

Meetro may provide AI-assisted features that help users understand workflows, summarize information, draft messages, organize service details, suggest next steps, and support professional operations.

AI assistance is informational and supportive only.

## AI Is Not Final Authority

AI-generated output does not replace user judgment, professional judgment, legal advice, safety review, licensing requirements, contractual obligations, or final business decisions.

Users remain responsible for reviewing, editing, approving, and verifying AI-generated content before relying on it or sending it to others.

## Possible AI Limitations

AI-assisted features may:

- Misunderstand user intent
- Produce incomplete or inaccurate suggestions
- Miss context
- Summarize information imperfectly
- Suggest wording or next steps that require human review
- Reflect limitations in the information provided by users

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Reviewing AI-generated messages, summaries, quotes, notes, and recommendations
- Confirming details before approval, scheduling, payment, or work decisions
- Avoiding reliance on AI for legal, medical, financial, emergency, compliance, or safety-critical decisions
- Correcting inaccurate or incomplete AI output

## Professional Responsibility

Professionals remain responsible for:

- Their services
- Their quotes and proposals
- Their business representations
- Their communications
- Their compliance with laws, licenses, permits, insurance, and professional standards

## No Professional Advice

Meetro AI assistance does not provide legal, financial, medical, engineering, construction, licensing, insurance, tax, or regulatory advice.

When needed, users should consult qualified professionals or appropriate authorities.

## Platform Role

Meetro provides AI-assisted workflow tools to improve communication and organization. AI assistance does not create contracts, approve work, authorize closure, verify compliance, guarantee outcomes, or replace human decision-making.
