# Meetro Emotional Storytelling Standard

## Purpose

Every visual element inside Meetro should communicate why meaningful work matters.

Users should not simply admire the project.

They should imagine the life that became possible because of the project.

## The Law of Living Photography

Never photograph the construction first.

Photograph the life that followed.

The emotional outcome is always more important than the physical improvement.

## Show People

Whenever possible, show people.

A completed home without people is architecture.

A completed home with people becomes a story.

## The Question Every Image Must Answer

Every image should answer:

What became possible because this work was completed?

Do not lead with:

- What was built?
- What was installed?
- What was repaired?

Lead with:

- What changed?
- Who felt safer?
- Who gathered?
- Who trusted someone more?
- What life became possible?

## Photography Hierarchy

### Tier 1: People Experiencing Life

Use first whenever available.

Examples:

- family dinner
- children playing
- couple relaxing
- friends celebrating
- neighbor helping neighbor
- business owner thanking customer

### Tier 2: Relationship Moments

Use when life-after-the-work imagery is not available.

Examples:

- handshake
- conversation
- planning together
- celebrating completion
- customer and professional trust

### Tier 3: Finished Space

Use only after people or relationship imagery.

Examples:

- kitchen
- pergola
- roof
- landscape
- bathroom

The finished space supports the story.

It is not the story.

## Never Lead With

- tools
- equipment
- construction
- workers posing
- empty rooms
- technical details

Those can support proof.

They should not lead the emotional surface.

## The Meetro Question

Every photograph must answer:

Why did someone hire this professional?

Correct answers:

- to create memories
- to protect family
- to improve life
- to strengthen relationships
- to build trust
- to make home feel like home

Services are not outcomes.

## Storytelling Examples

Incorrect:

Kitchen Remodel

Correct:

The kitchen where Sunday dinners began.

Incorrect:

Pergola Installation

Correct:

Summer evenings became family traditions.

Incorrect:

Roof Replacement

Correct:

Another family stayed safe through every storm.

Incorrect:

Bathroom Remodel

Correct:

The morning routine became peaceful again.

Incorrect:

Holiday Lighting

Correct:

The children could not wait to come home.

## The Emotion Test

Before approving photography, ask:

Would this make someone imagine their future?

Or would this simply show completed work?

If it only shows completed work, choose another image.

## The Meetro Promise

Meetro does not celebrate construction.

Meetro celebrates what construction makes possible.

That is the difference between documenting projects and preserving life.

The Lantern stays lit.

And is never forgotten.
