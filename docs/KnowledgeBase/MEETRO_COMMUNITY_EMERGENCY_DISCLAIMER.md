# Meetro Community Emergency Disclaimer

Last Updated: June 2026

Meetro may provide emergency-related communication, dispatch, workflow, and status tools.

Meetro is not a replacement for emergency services.

If there is immediate danger, a medical emergency, fire, active threat, gas leak, electrical hazard, flooding risk, structural danger, or any situation requiring urgent public safety response, users should call 911 or the appropriate local emergency authority immediately.

## Platform Role

Meetro may help users:

- Communicate emergency service needs
- Share location, photos, notes, and access details
- Connect with available professionals when supported
- Track emergency workflow status
- Preserve emergency-related records

Meetro does not guarantee:

- Emergency response availability
- Professional acceptance
- Arrival time
- Safety outcome
- Resolution of the emergency
- Accuracy of user-submitted emergency information

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Prioritizing personal safety
- Contacting public emergency services when appropriate
- Providing accurate information
- Following instructions from emergency authorities
- Avoiding unsafe repairs or hazardous conditions

## Professional Responsibility

Professionals are responsible for:

- Evaluating whether they are qualified and able to respond
- Following applicable laws, licensing rules, safety requirements, and industry standards
- Communicating limitations, arrival expectations, and safety concerns
- Declining work they cannot safely or legally perform

## No Emergency Service Guarantee

Meetro provides technology tools. Meetro does not operate as a public emergency dispatch service, first responder agency, medical provider, utility authority, law enforcement agency, or disaster response authority.

Emergency-related features are support tools only and must not be relied upon as the sole method for obtaining emergency assistance.
