# Arrival Experience Wonder Pass

## Purpose

The login screen is the front door to Meetro Community.

Members should feel like they are returning to a familiar place where work,
relationships, and community continue.

## Visual Standard

The Arrival Experience should use the Meetro Community visual constitution:

- forest and deep forest for primary action
- sage, cream, paper, wood, and coffee support tones
- warm borders
- soft shadows
- quiet porch-light or neighborhood-arrival atmosphere

It should not feel like a generic SaaS login or a purple technology platform.

## Copy Standard

Preferred arrival language:

- Welcome back.
- Continue the work that matters.
- Sign in to continue where you left off.
- Join Meetro Community.
- Continue.

Avoid:

- The modern platform for home and business services.
- marketing-heavy platform language
- cold technical entry copy

## Guardrails

- Do not change authentication behavior.
- Do not change signup behavior.
- Do not change language selection behavior.
- Do not change forgot password behavior.
- Do not change legal routing.
- Do not change backend/API calls.
- Do not change validation.
- Do not add auth features.
